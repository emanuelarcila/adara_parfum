# ADARA PARFUM — DESIGN SYSTEM

## 1. Brand Identity

Brand: ADARA PARFUM

Creative direction: Obsidian & Gold.

Create a premium contemporary fragrance e-commerce experience.

The brand should communicate:
- Exclusivity
- Sophistication
- Sensuality
- Confidence
- Editorial elegance
- Modern luxury

The experience must feel art-directed rather than template-generated.

Avoid generic e-commerce aesthetics.

## 2. Color System

Define reusable CSS variables.

Primary background: #0B0B0A
Secondary background: #151513
Primary gold: #C6A15B
Secondary gold: #8E713D
Primary text: #F3EEE5
Secondary text: #AAA49A
Subtle border: #383024

Use gold as a deliberate accent.

Do not apply gold to every heading, paragraph, border or button.

Maintain sufficient contrast and readable text.

Avoid bright yellow, excessive gradients and artificial neon effects.

## 3. Typography

Display typography:
Cormorant Garamond or a suitable equivalent.

Functional typography:
Manrope or a suitable equivalent.

Use display typography for:
- Hero headings
- Editorial sections
- Brand statements
- Selected product titles

Use functional typography for:
- Navigation
- Buttons
- Product prices
- Forms
- Filters
- Cart
- Administrative interfaces

An optional script typeface may be used for a small brand signature.

Never sacrifice readability for decoration.

## 4. Layout

Use:
- Strong editorial hierarchy
- Intentional negative space
- Carefully aligned content
- Asymmetric compositions where appropriate
- High-quality product photography
- Restrained decorative details
- Consistent spacing
- Responsive layouts

Avoid:
- Generic card grids everywhere
- Excessive rounded containers
- Unnecessary glassmorphism
- Random decorative gradients
- Repetitive sections
- Oversized text without purpose
- Excessive shadows

## 5. Photography

Product photography is a central part of the experience.

Prioritize:
- Controlled studio lighting
- Deep shadows
- Refined reflections
- Dark editorial compositions
- Accurate product proportions
- Appropriate image crops

Never distort product packaging or invent product labels.

Use only authorized assets or properly licensed imagery.

Use clearly identified placeholders until genuine business assets
are available.

## 6. Motion Design

Motion should feel elegant, controlled and intentional.

Implement:
- Staggered hero entrance
- Subtle product image transitions
- Smooth navigation states
- Refined cart drawer transitions
- Scroll-triggered reveals where appropriate
- Button hover and focus feedback
- Clear loading and success states

Prefer transform and opacity animations.

Avoid:
- Excessive bouncing
- Constant movement
- Distracting parallax
- Unnecessary animation libraries
- Animations that delay shopping interactions

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Ensure animations work on mobile devices.

## 7. Product Experience

Products must remain the visual priority.

Show:
- Product image
- Product name
- Brand
- Presentation or size
- Price in COP
- Availability
- Clear purchase action

Provide useful search, filtering and sorting.

Do not invent product information.

Do not make unsupported authenticity or commercial claims.

## 8. Responsive Design

Design mobile layouts deliberately.

Do not simply shrink the desktop interface.

Verify:
- Navigation
- Product grids
- Product details
- Cart
- Forms
- Buttons
- Image cropping
- Typography
- Touch targets

## 9. Accessibility

Use semantic HTML.

Maintain visible keyboard focus.

Ensure readable contrast.

Provide meaningful image alternative text.

Respect reduced-motion preferences.

Do not use color as the only indicator of status.

## 10. Performance

Optimize images.

Avoid unnecessary dependencies.

Prefer efficient animations.

Prevent layout shifts.

Do not sacrifice usability for visual effects.

## 11. Consistency

All components must follow this design system.

Use shared design tokens.

Do not introduce arbitrary colors, fonts, spacing values or animation
patterns without a clear reason.

If a change to the design system is necessary, update this document.

## 12. Quality Gate

Before considering a page complete, verify:

- Visual consistency
- Responsive behavior
- Typography
- Spacing
- Image quality
- Hover states
- Focus states
- Loading states
- Error states
- Accessibility
- Animation performance

The final result must feel intentionally designed for ADARA PARFUM,
not generated from a generic storefront template.
